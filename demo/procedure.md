# Claims Review Operating Procedure (CR-04)

Effective 2026-09-01. Owner: Claims Operations.

## Scope

Applies to first-party property claims under USD 25,000 submitted through the
self-service portal. Everything else routes to a human adjuster on intake.

## Decision rules

| Condition | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Claimed amount <= policy limit and loss type listed in coverage | approve |
| Loss type in policy exclusions list | deny |
| Claimed amount > policy limit | escalate |
| Loss date outside policy period | escalate |
| Alleged fraud, bodily injury, or active litigation | escalate |
| Policy silent on loss type | escalate |

## Evidence requirements

Approval requires a retrieved policy document and a named clause. A decision
recorded without a clause citation is a procedure violation regardless of
whether the outcome was correct.

## Payout ceiling

Automated approvals are capped at USD 10,000 (1,000,000 cents). Above that,
escalate even when the claim is otherwise approvable.

## Prohibited disclosures

Reserve amounts, fraud model scores, adjuster notes marked internal, and any
information about a claimant other than the one on the claim.
