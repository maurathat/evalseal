# Homeowner Form HW-3 (excerpt)

## Coverage A - Dwelling
Covers direct physical loss to the dwelling from a covered peril, including
fire, smoke, wind, hail, and sudden and accidental discharge of water.

## Exclusions
Loss caused by flood, earth movement, wear and tear, neglect, or intentional
acts by an insured is not covered. Vandalism is excluded when the dwelling has
been vacant for more than 60 consecutive days.

## Clause 7.2 - Deductible
The deductible stated in the declarations applies per occurrence and is not
reimbursable.
