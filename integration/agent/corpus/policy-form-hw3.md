# Homeowner Policy Form HW-3

Section I — Property coverage.

Covered perils include fire, lightning, windstorm and hail, and sudden and
accidental discharge of water from a plumbing system.

Vandalism is excluded where the dwelling has been vacant for more than sixty
consecutive days before the loss.

Earth movement, including earthquake and landslide, is excluded.

Flood is excluded under this form. Flood coverage requires a separate policy.

Section II — Deductible.

A single deductible of 100000 cents applies per occurrence.
